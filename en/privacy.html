Privacy

Privacy

The principle is simple: what can stay on your machine stays on your machine; we only handle information you hand over on purpose; your data is not traded for anything else.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Conversations stay on your machine by default

  • Chark's model runs on your own machine. Conversations are saved locally by default and are not automatically uploaded to a server you have not authorized.
  • The few capabilities that need a network explain what they use the network for when you use them; you can choose not to use them.
  • The files you hand over are read and processed only on this machine and are not kept extra.

About this site

  • This is a plain static site. It does not use traffic analytics, advertising pixels, or third-party tracking scripts.
  • This site does not set cookies used to identify an individual.
  • Styles and scripts are hosted on this site itself; no resources are requested from an external content delivery network.
  • The site keeps the only necessary logging capability on by default: the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) processes requests to run the service; this part is outside the site's control.

About email

  • Welcome emails are sent through the email service Brevo. To deliver the message, Brevo processes the recipient address, delivery status, and bounce information.
  • Images in the email are hosted in this site's public directory and load from the site when the email is opened. The mail client may block remote images by default; this does not affect the email body.
  • We do not use email addresses for profiling or advertising, and we do not hand them to third parties unrelated to delivery.
  • You can stop receiving product emails at any time through the email preferences page or by replying to the email.

What information we process

  • What you provide: the recipient email, the content of emails you send, and the files you give to Chark.
  • Required to run the service: minimal records such as email delivery status.
  • What we do not collect: browsing history unrelated to using the product, contacts, device fingerprints, or your data from other services.

What you can request

  • Access: confirm whether we still keep your email or old email records.
  • Deletion: request deletion of the email content you sent and locally kept files.
  • Stop mailing: stop receiving product emails at any time.

Send any of these requests to chen@chenc.com.cn; it will be handled and replied to as soon as possible.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes materially, the date at the top of this page will be updated; adjustments that affect your rights will be mentioned separately in an email.

FAQ

What is Chark?+

Chark is an AI trained locally by ChenC, with chat, writing, code, image understanding, file analysis, and offline inference. The little shark is its character; the AI model is the core of the product.

Where can I use it?+

Chark currently runs as a local model. Its user-facing experience is still in development. This page introduces capabilities and interface concepts; a new AI app download or web chat is not available here yet.

Does offline use send content to the cloud?+

Offline inference runs on your machine, without needing to send conversations, images, or files to a cloud model service. This introduction page does not read or upload your local material either.

How does the little shark relate to Chark?+

The shark is Chark’s character, and desktop companionship is one way to interact with it. Chark itself is the locally trained AI, supporting conversation, writing, code, image understanding, and file analysis.

What can image and file analysis do?+

Ask questions about images and documents, organize information, summarize important details, and continue a conversation with context. Check small text, complex tables, formulas, and important conclusions. The examples on this page are fictional.

Does this page access my files?+

No. The filenames, articles, memories, and scenarios are invented for this demonstration. They do not come from the creator’s or visitors’ computers, chats, or research. Interactions do not capture your screen, upload files, contact a model, or read local documents.

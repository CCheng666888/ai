Help

Chark is an AI trained locally by ChenC. This page explains how to get started, what it can do, and who to contact if you run into a problem.

Getting started

  • Once your account is ready, you will receive a welcome email with a button that opens Chark. Just click it.
  • If images in the email appear blank, the mail client is likely blocking remote images by default; click "Show images" to display them.
  • The unified entry point is still in development. Until it opens, the button in the email points back to this site's home page. To try Chark early, write directly to chen@chenc.com.cn.

What Chark can do

  • Chat: explain your question clearly and it will work through the reasoning step by step.
  • Writing: from outline to draft; adjust tone, length, and structure.
  • Code: read code, write code, and explain errors.
  • Image understanding: make sense of information in screenshots, charts, and photos.
  • File analysis: read the documents you hand over and summarize the key points.
  • Offline inference: the model runs on your own machine and works without a network connection.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to stay online to use Chark?

No. The model runs locally, so you can keep chatting and writing offline. Only a few online-only capabilities (such as looking up the latest information) will ask you to connect to the network.

The button in the welcome email won't open — what should I do?

First try copying the link from the email into your browser. If it still won't open, send the email subject and a screenshot to chen@chenc.com.cn and it will be handled as soon as possible.

Can I use a different email address?

Yes. Write to the support address with your old and new email addresses. Changing the email does not affect the account itself.

I no longer want to receive emails — how do I stop?

Follow the steps on the email preferences page, or simply reply to any Chark email with "unsubscribe". Required account notices (such as security alerts) will be explained separately.

Will my questions be used to train the model?

No. Your conversations stay on your machine by default; the exact boundaries are described in the Privacy page.

I found a wrong answer — how do I report it?

Send the question, the answer you expected, and the actual output to chen@chenc.com.cn. Including the time helps locate the issue faster.

Contact support

Email: chen@chenc.com.cn
When writing, please include the email address you use and the specific issue you ran into, so it can be located quickly.
